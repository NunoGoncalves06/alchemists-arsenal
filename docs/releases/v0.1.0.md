The first playable build of Alchemist's Arsenal.

Brew bombs by hand in the morning, send your fighters down the road in the afternoon, and spend what they bring back in the evening. All five roads are in, from the Whispering Woods to the last one, along with the story, the diary and three save slots.

**Download:** `AlchemistsArsenal-Windows.zip` below. Unzip it, keep the files together, and run `AlchemistsArsenal.exe`. Windows may warn that it protected your PC because the game isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a DirectX 11 graphics card.
