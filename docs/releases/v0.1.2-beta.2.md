A second test build of 0.1.2, with two fixes from the testers. It is a pre-release; your saves carry over.

**Fixed**

- The tutorial no longer strands you at the cork. If a cork glanced off the neck, the lesson kept lighting the empty dish, and you could not click the cork where it was waiting. The lesson now lights the cork wherever it is.
- A flask handed to a fighter now hangs at their waist. It used to float over their head at full size, which made the hand-over look unfinished.

Everything in 0.1.2 beta is still here: the cauldron with momentum, the green and yellow zones on every bench, the new title screen and the pictures in the diary.

**Download**

- Windows: `AlchemistsArsenal-Windows.zip`. Unzip it, keep the files together, and run `AlchemistsArsenal.exe`. Windows may warn that it protected your PC because the game isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Linux: `AlchemistsArsenal-Linux.tar.gz`. Unpack it with `tar -xzf` and run `./AlchemistsArsenal/AlchemistsArsenal.x86_64`.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with a DirectX 11 graphics card, or Linux x86_64 with Vulkan or OpenGL 4.5.
