A test build of what is coming in 0.1.2, made from the testers' feedback. It is a pre-release: things may still change before the official 0.1.2, and your saves carry over.

**What's new**

- The cauldron has momentum. Push the brew round in an arc and it keeps turning by itself, slowing down until you push again. The gauge shows which way to turn and warns you before the brew sticks or slops over.
- Every bench gauge has a narrow green zone in the middle of its band. A step in the green pays the most, a step in the yellow around it still counts but pays less. A flask at 100 now needs a morning kept on the green, and the green narrows from the second road on.
- The machines hold the green. The Auto Crusher, Auto Doser, Draught Kiln and Turning Vat always work dead on, and the Clockwork Stirrer brews better than a hand that only keeps the yellow.
- A new title screen: the shop with Nell and the fighters, and a card that shows where your last run stands (day, road and gold) and continues it in one click.
- The diary's road pages show the people in them, and Nell climbs to the summit behind her fighters before the mask breaks.
- The kiln no longer charges "gone cool" before it has ever been warm.
- On Linux, the README says where your saves are kept.

**Download**

- Windows: `AlchemistsArsenal-Windows.zip`. Unzip it, keep the files together, and run `AlchemistsArsenal.exe`. Windows may warn that it protected your PC because the game isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Linux: `AlchemistsArsenal-Linux.tar.gz`. Unpack it with `tar -xzf` and run `./AlchemistsArsenal/AlchemistsArsenal.x86_64`.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with a DirectX 11 graphics card, or Linux x86_64 with Vulkan or OpenGL 4.5.
