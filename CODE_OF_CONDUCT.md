# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part here, in issues, discussions or pull requests, is expected to be respectful.

Be kind and assume good faith. Criticize work, not people. Keep feedback specific and useful.

Harassment, insults, slurs, personal attacks, sexual content, and publishing someone's private information are not allowed, and neither is trolling or deliberately derailing a conversation.

If someone breaks these rules, their comments may be edited or removed, and they may be blocked from the repository.

To report a problem, open an issue. If it is about a specific person or should stay private, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment in question, which goes to GitHub and to the maintainer.
